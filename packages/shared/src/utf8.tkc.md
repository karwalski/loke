Cuts text to a BYTE limit without splitting a UTF-8 character (F10.48, F10.49). `boundary(s;limit)` is the largest cut at or before `limit` that does not fall inside a character: it moves back while the byte at the cut is a continuation byte (10xxxxxx), at most three steps; a limit past the end gives the length and a negative one gives 0. `prefix(s;limit)` is `s` cut there, with no marker added. `valid(s)` is a structural UTF-8 check (every lead byte has its continuation bytes, no stray continuation byte), used by the callers' tests to show a cut output is whole; it does not reject overlong forms or surrogates. Depends on `std.str`.
