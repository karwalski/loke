External observability integrations management command. Lists configured monitoring/alerting endpoints, adds preset integrations (PagerDuty, Datadog, Grafana, Splunk, Elastic, Slack, Teams, OpsGenie, ServiceNow, webhook), and sends test events to verify endpoint connectivity. Exports run, printusage, runlist, runadd, runtest, buildpresetendpoint, presetnames, and isvalidpreset functions. Depends on std.str, shared.log, cli.output, core.governance.external, shared.config, loke.platform.i18n.translator, std.http, and std.time.
