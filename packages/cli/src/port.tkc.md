Port management for the loke proxy server. Checks port availability by attempting to bind, finds free ports in the 11430--11450 range, reads and persists the configured port to the database, and resolves conflicts by falling back to an available port with a warning. Exports defaultport, portrangestart, portrangeend, isportfree, findfreeport, getconfiguredport, saveport, and resolveport functions. Depends on shared.types, shared.log, cli.output, std.net, storage.db, and std.str.
