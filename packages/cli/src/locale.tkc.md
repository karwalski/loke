CLI locale management with list and validate subcommands. Lists available locales (en-AU, en-US) with an active marker, and validates that all translation keys in the baseline locale (en-AU) exist in other locales, reporting any missing keys. Exports run, printusage, runlist, and runvalidate functions. Depends on loke.platform.i18n.translator, loke.platform.i18n.locales, std.str, and shared.log.
