CLI interface for governance policy management with list, validate, and show subcommands. Lists active policy sources and merge order, validates policy files against the schema reporting errors and warnings, and displays policy details including providers, anonymisation rules, and cross-border settings. Exports run, printusage, runlist, runvalidate, and runshow functions. Depends on core.policy.schema, core.policy.loader, core.policy.validate, std.str, shared.log, and loke.platform.i18n.translator.
