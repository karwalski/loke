Audit metrics aggregation over `metrics_raw`, the table `core.metrics.collector` writes and `page.api.pipeline` fills with one row per dispatch and per cache hit (DA1.3, DA1.6). `periodstart` resolves a period (`all`, `24h`, `7d`, `30d`, `90d`) to a unix start, and returns -1 for anything else. `summaryfrom`, `byproviderfrom`, `seriesfrom`, `cachefrom` and `exportfrom` (csv or json) are plain functions over a resolved start. `summary`, `byprovider`, `tokensavingsseries`, `cachestats` and `export` are the result-returning wrappers, and they refuse an unknown period or format. Cost is summed as integer micro-dollars. `$cacheinfo` carries only hits and misses, because metrics_raw records nothing else about the cache. Exports summary, emptysummary, byprovider, tokensavingsseries, cachestats, emptycachestats, export and the plain twins. Depends on shared.types, std.db, and std.str. Tested by tests/integration/test_audit_metrics.tk.
