Per-layer privacy metrics in the audit trail (F3b.10). `record` writes one request's `privacy_layer_metrics` rows (migration 41) in one savepoint, once per correlation id; `core.storage.audit.insertbound` binds their digest into every audit row on that id (`layer_digest`, migration 43), and `verifychain` recomputes it. `bylayerday`, `bytype` and `agreementsince` aggregate the rows by SQL for a dashboard: detections by layer per UTC day, by entity type, and the share of resolved detections every voting layer found, over runs with two or more voters.
