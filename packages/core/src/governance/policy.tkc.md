Governance policy evaluation. Classifies request risk by combining data sensitivity, use-case risk tier, and estimated cost into a final risk tier, then evaluates policy decisions (allow, allow with warning, require approval, deny) based on that risk. Also determines which controls (anonymisation, audit logging, human-visible preview, local-only routing, etc.) should be applied. Exports classifyrisk, evaluate, and controlsforrisk. Depends on shared.types, core.governance.types, std.str, and shared.log.
