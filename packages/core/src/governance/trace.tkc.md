End-to-end decision tracing. Records a full audit trace for each request including PII detection, anonymisation, risk classification, policy decision, model selection, provider routing, prompt sent, response received, and de-anonymisation. Supports step-by-step trace building, JSON serialisation with safe escaping and truncation, and human-readable summaries. Exports newtrace, addstep, recordpii, recordrouting, recordresponse, complete, tojson, and summary. Depends on shared.types, core.governance.types, std.str, and shared.log.
