Builds a governance scorecard with RAG-status metrics (privacy coverage, local routing ratio, cost, open incidents, audit trail integrity) from `audit_log`, and formats it as a fixed-width text dashboard. The period window is resolved to epoch milliseconds by `core.storage.db.daysagoms` and bound as a parameter against a CAST column (GA5.11); comparing `created_at` to `datetime('now','-N days')` was a string comparison that matched nothing. Sensitivity is compared case-insensitively, because the column is written in lower case. Two metrics that were previously hardcoded are now measured or withdrawn: open incidents is counted from the `incidents` table, and "governance coverage", which was the literal 1.0 and therefore GREEN on every installation, is replaced by an audit trail integrity metric derived from `audit.verifychain` and the append-only triggers. A ratio over zero events reports "no events" in AMBER instead of 100%. Depends on shared.types, core.storage.db, core.storage.audit, std.db, core.governance.types, std.str and shared.log.
