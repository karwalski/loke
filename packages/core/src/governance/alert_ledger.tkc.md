De-duplicated governance alerts (F3b.8 (6)). `raise(kind;severity;message)` sends an alert (core.governance.alert) the first time its kind, severity and message are seen and then at most once an hour (`windowms`) while they recur, counting every occurrence in `governance_alert_ledger` (migration 44) with first, last and last-sent times; `raiseat` takes the time and window, for tests. Fails towards alerting: when the ledger cannot be read or written the alert is sent. `seensince(kind;sincems)` lists what was seen in a window, for the governance page. Callers pass messages that hold no user text (core.privacy.pipeline: layer ids, settings and policy values).
