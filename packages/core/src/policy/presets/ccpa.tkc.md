Built-in policy preset for the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act. Anonymises names, email, SSN, financial, biometric and geolocation data; permits cross-border transfer; requires approval above the confidential level. Exports `preset()` and `rules()` (5 `$policyrule`s, the two migrated from the deleted `core.policy.regulations.ccpa()` plus the service-provider contract, 45-day response and sensitive-PI opt-in rules its `customrules` already named). Both budget limits are 0 because `docs/specifications/policy-examples` holds no CCPA or CPRA policy example, so no source document in this repository gives a figure for this preset. Depends on `core.policy.schema` and `core.policy.types`.
