Built-in policy preset for EU GDPR (2016/679). Blocks health, biometric, political, and religion data; applies full anonymisation to all personal identifiers; disables cross-border transfer; and enforces DPA requirements and right-to-erasure support. Exports `preset()` and `rules()` (7 `$policyrule`s, the five migrated from the deleted `core.policy.regulations.gdpr()` plus the Article 9 and DPA rules its `customrules` already named). `maxcostusd` is 0.0 because the source document's figure, in `docs/specifications/policy-examples/eu-gdpr.yaml`, is EUR 100 monthly and this field is USD-denominated. Depends on `core.policy.schema` and `core.policy.types`.
