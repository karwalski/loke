Built-in policy preset for the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the 13 Australian Privacy Principles, including the reforms introduced by the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment (Tranche 1) Act 2024 (story RG1.3). Exports `preset()` and `rules()` (21 `$policyrule`s). Depends on `core.policy.schema` and `core.policy.types`.

Source for every reform rule is `docs/specifications/regulatory-defaults.md` section 3, which is the only in-repository source that states them: section 3.1 for the statutory tort for serious invasion of privacy, the Children's Online Privacy Code, the enhanced OAIC enforcement powers, the criminalisation of doxxing and the expanded definition of personal information; section 3.2 and 3.3 for inferred data being in scope, which makes outbound response scanning a requirement rather than a nicety; section 3.4 for the re-identification prohibition; section 3.5 for retention; section 3.9 for the 30-day Notifiable Data Breaches window. `docs/specifications/policy-examples/au-privacy-act.yaml` supplies the government-identifier and credential handling.

Three substantive changes beyond adding rules. Sensitive information (health, genetic, biometric, racial, political, religious, sexual orientation, trade union, criminal) is now `block` rather than `anonymise`, per section 3.4, which applies the same irreversible redaction as the EU GDPR preset. `piimappingdays` is 2 rather than 30: section 3.5 puts mapping tables at 48 hours after session close, and a mapping table held for a month under an Act whose 2024 amendments reinforce the prohibition on re-identification is a month of re-identification capability. The government identifiers and credentials named in the YAML — driver's licence, passport, Centrelink CRN, BSB, bank account, superannuation member number, credit card, API key, password — are blocked, and `rules()` states plainly that loke has a detector for none of the Australian ones (AD1.1), so the block cannot fire on a value it never sees.

The Children's Online Privacy Code is present as a rule marked `NOT IMPLEMENTED` rather than omitted: loke has no age signal, no child-user determination and no code-specific handling. `maxcostusd` is 0.0 because the source document's figure is AUD 150 monthly and this field is USD-denominated; converting it would put an unmeasured number in a regulatory preset.
