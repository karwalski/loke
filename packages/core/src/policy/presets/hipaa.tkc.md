Built-in policy preset for US HIPAA. Blocks PHI, SSN, medical record and insurance identifiers; requires a business associate agreement before any provider receives PHI; retains audit records for six years. Exports `preset()` and `rules()` (6 `$policyrule`s, the three migrated from the deleted `core.policy.regulations.hipaa()` plus the BAA, safe-harbor and breach-notification rules its `customrules` already named). `maxcostusd` is 100.0, taken from `docs/specifications/policy-examples/hipaa.yaml`, whose monthly global limit is stated in USD and so needs no conversion; `maxtokens` stays 0 because the source gives no token figure. The safe-harbor rule states that loke does not guarantee complete detection of the eighteen identifier categories, so the pipeline is evidence toward the standard and not a determination of it. Depends on `core.policy.schema` and `core.policy.types`.
