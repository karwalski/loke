Per-layer privacy filter metrics (F3b.10). `forlayer` reduces what one detection layer reported for one text to a layer row (state, mode, measured milliseconds, detection and accepted counts, confidence minimum, maximum and sum in thousandths) and one row per entity type. `agreed` and `countaction` summarise the consensus record, and `summaryjson` renders the `privacy_layers`, `consensus` and `vetoed_by` fields of `/api/pipeline`'s reply. No detected value, and no hash of one, enters a row or the summary. Called by `core.privacy.pipeline.run`; stored by `core.audit.layermetrics`.
