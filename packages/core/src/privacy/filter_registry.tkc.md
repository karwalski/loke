Privacy filter registry. Manages a registry of privacy detection filters (regex, NER, Presidio) with their entity type coverage, confidence thresholds, and enabled/disabled state. Supports registering, listing, filtering to enabled-only, and looking up filters by ID. Provides a default registry with regex and NER enabled and Presidio disabled. Exports newregistry, register, get, list, listenabled, and defaultregistry. Depends on shared.types and std.str.
