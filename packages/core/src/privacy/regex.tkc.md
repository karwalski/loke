Regex-based PII detection layer. getpatterns declares twenty-two pattern definitions (the pattern strings are in core.privacy.patterns) with their sensitivity levels and confidence scores. Runs every pattern against the input text, recovers each match's span, rejects a match with a digit next to it for digit-run patterns, and with a letter next to it for au_tfn (after only: RG1.7b, so the nine checksum-valid digits of a part number such as 984593866N, or of a CRN, are not a TFN) and for the NINO, GB driver number and NRIC (after; before as well for the two unlabelled ones). A context match (BSB, account, CRN, passport, licence, super member, NHS, sort code, NRIC/FIN) reports the identifier alone, not its words. Validation: tfnchecksumvalid (TFN weights mod 11), medicarechecksumvalid, bsbvalid, nhschecksumvalid (NHS Data Dictionary modulus 11; a check of 10 is never issued), ninovalid (HMRC NIM39110 prefix and suffix rules), sortcodevalid (rejects only 00-00-00), ukdrivinglicencevalid (DVLA INS57P structure; the check characters are not judged). The NRIC check letter is not validated: its algorithm is not published by the ICA. Tracks the highest sensitivity level found and returns a detection result whose placeholders and anonymised text come from core.privacy.placeholder. Exports getpatterns, detect, detectwithpatterns and the validation predicates. Depends on shared.types, core.privacy.patterns, core.privacy.placeholder, and std.str.
