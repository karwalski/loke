Organisation privacy policy (F3b.8). Reads `loke.policy.json` in the user's loke directory (`policypath`; `load`, `fromtext`): `minimum_layers`, `required_layers`, `locked_consensus` (only `most-restrictive`), `banned_layers` (not `regex`), `max_confidence_threshold`, `enforced`, `description`. A missing file is no policy (`absent`); a file that cannot be read, is not a JSON object, names an unknown or repeated key, or gives an unusable value is `broken`, and its `error` refuses every request. `apply` puts a policy over one run's registry and consensus settings: a weaker consensus strategy is raised (recorded in `raisedfrom` and `notes`), thresholds above the maximum are clamped, a banned layer is switched off and, if the configuration enabled it, sets `error`. `refusal` says why a run is refused once its layers have answered: the policy's error, a required layer that is off, detect-only or did not scan, or fewer masking layers than `minimum_layers`. A policy can only make privacy stricter. Called by `core.privacy.pipeline.run` on every run. Depends on `std.str`, `std.json`, `std.file`, `shared.jsonobj`, `shared.types`, `shared.config`, `core.privacy.filterregistry`, `core.privacy.consensus`, `core.privacy.layerhealth`.
