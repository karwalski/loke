Multi-layer detection consensus (F3b.4). `core.privacy.pipeline.run` calls `applyranked` (`apply` with each detection's sensitivity rank) on every layer detection before dedup: detections are grouped (overlapping spans, or equal values, transitively), the layers that completed a scan are the voters, and `privacy.consensus_strategy` decides each group — `most-restrictive` (the union, default), `first-match`, `unanimous`, `majority` or `confidence-weighted` against `privacy.consensus_threshold`. Any other name, empty or mis-cased, is applied as `most-restrictive` (fail closed). Each detection is returned as a `$resolvedentity` recording the layers and confidences of its group and the action taken. When a group's most sensitive detection is not masked (first-match) and another of the group is, the masked one carries the most sensitive type (RG1.7c). Exports `apply`, `applyranked`, `resolvewith`, `resolveoverlaps`, `effective`, `parsethreshold`, `strategyname`. Depends on `std.str`, `shared.types`.
