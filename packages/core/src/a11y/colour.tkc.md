Reduced-motion and focus-visible CSS, and the WCAG 2.1 contrast ratio computed from relative luminance (the legacy checkcontrastratio was a stub returning 4.5 for every pair). Unparseable colours give 0.0 and fail meetsaa. Ported from src/platform/a11y/colour.tk (F10.12-T2). Exports motioncss, focusvisiblecss, checkcontrastratio, meetsaa and the hex helpers. Depends on std.str and std.math.
