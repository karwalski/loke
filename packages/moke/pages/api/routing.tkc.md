Routing API endpoint that builds a routing decision and tier visualisation for a given prompt and sensitivity level; its `cost` is `{"measured":false,...}`, because no token is counted before dispatch (GA1.5c: it was priced over a fixed 500 tokens in and 200 out). Note that the decision does not depend on the prompt: the intent is hard-coded to data_analysis, the tier to "interactive" and the hardware summary to an Apple M-series string, and both `prompt` and `tolerance` are read from the body and unused — only sensitivity and model_id reach the answer. Depends on std.http, std.json, std.str, std.time, shared.jsonobj, moke.routingexplainer and moke.tiervisualiser; the moke.companionsimulator and (GA1.5c) moke.costcomparison imports are gone.
