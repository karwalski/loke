Routing API endpoint that builds a routing decision and tier visualisation for a request's `prompt`, `sensitivity`, `tolerance` and `model_id`; its `cost` is `{"measured":false,...}`, because no token is counted before dispatch (GA1.5c: it was priced over a fixed 500 tokens in and 200 out). Since LS1.4a the prompt and tolerance reach the answer through moke.routingrequest: the intent is core.router.intent's keyword classification of the prompt, the sensitivity is the caller's level raised by the prompt's content, and the tier and `latency_tolerance` follow the tolerance; an unknown tolerance or sensitivity is 400 (before, the intent was always data_analysis / 0.85 and the tier interactive). Still not computed: the model is the caller's `model_id` and the provider is always local ollama (no model is selected; moke holds no registry), and the hardware summary is a fixed Apple M-series string. Depends on std.http, std.json, std.str, std.time, shared.jsonobj, moke.routingexplainer, moke.routingrequest and moke.tiervisualiser.
