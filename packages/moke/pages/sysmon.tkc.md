System monitor endpoint for moke. Answers loke's own /sysmon (fetched over loopback with shared.reachability.localget) when loke gives one; otherwise measures the machine through core.monitoring.sysmon.snapshotof(0), with the loke entry null because moke cannot name loke's process. Served as application/json. Depends on std.http, std.str, shared.reachability and core.monitoring.sysmon.
