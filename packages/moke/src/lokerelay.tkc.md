moke's same-origin relay to loke (BG1.4). `postto(base;path;body)` and `getfrom(base;path)` send a request unchanged to loke on loopback and answer a `$relayed{status;body}` carrying loke's own status and body, so a refusal (403, 409, 429) reaches the page as loke wrote it. They use http.postheaders and http.clientget, which carry the status at toke 3.1.0; shared.reachability.localpost would have answered "" for any non-2xx. A base that is not a loopback URL, nothing listening, a transport failure or a non-UTF-8 body answers `noanswer()`, a 502 with `"relayed_by":"moke"`. Nothing is filtered or decided here: loke's /api/pipeline runs the privacy pipeline on what it receives. Used by page.moke.api.pipeline (a loke-shaped body) and page.moke.api.models. Depends on std.http, std.str and shared.reachability.
