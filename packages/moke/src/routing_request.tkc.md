What moke's `/api/routing` takes from a request (LS1.4a), through loke's router modules: `inputs(prompt;sensitivity;tolerance)` answers a `$routeinputs` with the intent, confidence and method of `core.router.intent.classifyfast` on the prompt; the sensitivity, the caller's level as a floor raised by `core.router.sensitivity.scorefromcontent` on the prompt (combined as the router does), and its routing (`local-only` when the level may not use the cloud, else `prefer-local`); and the tier `core.router.selector` maps the latency tolerance onto (instant interactive, patient considered, background background). `toleranceknown` and `sensitivityknown` let the handler refuse a value the router does not know. It does not select a model: that needs `core.router.router.routerequest` and a registry moke does not hold. Only scalar fields leave the module, because shared.types and moke.routingexplainer both declare `$routingdecision`. Depends on std.str, core.router.intent, core.router.router, core.router.selector, core.router.sensitivity, shared.types.
