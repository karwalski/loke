Notification and toast system. Manages a store of notifications with support for create, markread, markallread, remove, and listing operations. Renders a bell icon with unread badge, a dropdown notification panel, and auto-dismissing toast popups with pause-on-hover behaviour. Exports types $notificationtype, $notification, $store, and functions newstore, create, markread, markallread, remove, unreadcount, list, renderbell, renderpanel, rendertoast, script (exposing window.lokeNotifications), and styles. Depends on std.str.
