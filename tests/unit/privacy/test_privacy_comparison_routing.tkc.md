Tests that `core.privacy.comparison` (`loke privacy evaluate`, F3b.9) decides as `core.privacy.pipeline.run` does now that run applies `[[privacy.entity_routing]]` (F3b.5). A drift guard runs `pl.run` and `cmp.compare` on the same text under eight configurations (default, majority, Presidio down, PERSON routed, routed under majority, fallback, neither layer up, malformed route) and compares every consensus decision (type, span, group layers with confidences, mask or pass), the strategy applied and the refusal. It also checks that the routing section of the report names which layers routing accepts PERSON from (preferred, fallback, or every layer that ran), lists the detections routing did not accept by type and span, and prints no value. `scripts/lib/http_recorder.py` stands in for NER and Presidio. Depends on `std.test`, `std.str`, `std.file`, `std.process`, `core.privacy.pipeline`, `core.privacy.comparison`, `core.privacy.evaluation`, `core.privacy.consensus`.
