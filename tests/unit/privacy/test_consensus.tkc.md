Tests `core.privacy.consensus` by importing it (F3b.4). Each of the five strategies is judged on one fixture where no two agree (an email found by two of three layers, a name by one); unanimity counts only the layers that voted; empty, unknown and mis-cased strategy names are applied as `most-restrictive`; each detection records the layers and confidences of its group; grouping follows overlapping spans and equal values transitively and does not join touching spans; the confidence threshold is read from text and clamped; `apply` keeps exactly the masked entities in input order. Depends on `std.test`, `std.str`, `shared.types`, `core.privacy.consensus`.
