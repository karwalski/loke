Tests that `core.privacy.pipeline.run` resolves its layers' detections through `core.privacy.consensus` (F3b.4), against `scripts/lib/http_recorder.py` standing in for NER and Presidio. Proves the default does not weaken detection: every value any single layer masks alone is masked by the default three-layer run, and it masks as many entities as the dedup of all layers' detections (the pre-consensus behaviour). Judges each strategy by the anonymised text, the per-entity provenance on `$pipelinerun.resolved`, and reading `privacy.consensus_strategy` and `privacy.consensus_threshold` from `config.toml`. Depends on `std.test`, `std.str`, `std.file`, `std.toml`, `std.process`, `core.privacy.pipeline`, `core.privacy.pipelinetypes`, `core.privacy.filterregistry`, `core.privacy.consensus`, `shared.config`, `shared.types`.
