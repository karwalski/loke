Tests the parsers behind `core.monitoring.sysmon` on canned vm_stat and top output (BG1.2): used RAM from active, wired and compressed pages at the page size vm_stat states (16 KiB and 4 KiB pages), the busy share from top's idle figure, and nothing measured when a listing is missing. Also reads pid 1 by pid and checks that an unmeasured loke entry serialises as null and that a live snapshot reports RAM in use. Before BG1.2 the parsers required one more field than their input has, so /sysmon reported 0 MB and 0% everywhere. Depends on `std.test`, `std.str` and `core.monitoring.sysmon`.
