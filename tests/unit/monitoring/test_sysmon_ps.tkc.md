Tests `core.monitoring.sysmon.getprocessstats` against the real `ps` on macOS. Three defects kept it from reading any process: a Linux-only `nlwp` column, waiting for `ps` before reading its output (a pipe deadlock that hung `/sysmon` intermittently), and a starting value that an idle process could never replace. Asserts that launchd is found as pid 1 and that a missing process reports 0. Depends on `std.test` and `core.monitoring.sysmon`.
