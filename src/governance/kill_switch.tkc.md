Emergency kill switch for disabling AI services at global, provider, or use-case scope. Maintains a list of active kill switch entries with reason and operator attribution, checks whether a given scope/target is blocked, and provides user-facing fallback messages. Exports killswitchentry, fallbackmessage, and killswitchstate types, plus enableglobal, disableglobal, enableprovider, enableusecase, isblocked, getfallbackmessage, and listactive. Depends on std.str and std.log.
