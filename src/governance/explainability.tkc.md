AI decision explainability for transparency and compliance. Generates plain-language explanations of how a request was processed (PII detection, anonymisation, compression, risk tier, model selection, entity restoration) from a decisiontrace. Supports both template-based local explanations and LLM-assisted explanations via Ollama. Exports decisiontrace and explanation types, plus explain, explainviallm, tracetojson, and tojson. Depends on std.log, std.string, std.http, and std.json.
