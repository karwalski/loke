Dockable chat panel UI for the browser workspace. Defines panelstate, transparencybar, and presendpreview types, and exports functions to show, hide, toggle, and dock the panel, append messages to history, build a transparency bar showing PII count/sensitivity/model/cost, render a pre-send preview comparing original and anonymised prompts with detected entities, and generate panel CSS. Depends on std.string, std.log, and std.str.
