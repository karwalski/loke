Privacy metadata extraction for web pages. Parses robots.txt directives to detect AI-training opt-outs (GPTBot, ClaudeBot), scans HTML for data-ai-sensitivity attributes, resolves page-level sensitivity (PUBLIC/INTERNAL/CONFIDENTIAL/RESTRICTED), and builds a pagemetadata record. Exports robotsdirective and pagemetadata types, along with parserobotstxt, detectdataaiattrs, resolvepagesensitivity, checkaidisallowed, buildmetadata, compliancenote, and tojson. Depends on std.string, std.log, and std.str.
