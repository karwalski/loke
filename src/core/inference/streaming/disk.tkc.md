Disk-streaming inference for models too large to fit in RAM. Loads model layers incrementally from NVMe shards, checks hardware compatibility, estimates tokens-per-second based on disk speed, and compares cost against cloud alternatives. Exports shardconfig, streamprogress, and streamresult types, plus defaultconfig, isavailable, estimatetokpersec, generate, cloudcostcomparison, and JSON serialisation functions. Depends on std.string, std.log, and loke.core.hardware.profile.
