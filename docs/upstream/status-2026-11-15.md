## What did not match toke's account

- **The spawn half of T-60 is fixed only for non-integers.** toke's reply and 3.2.0's breaking
  change 6 say opaque std handles are judged; they are, for a str, array, map or record. An i64
  is still accepted where `handle` is declared, and `process.wait(5)` dies with SIGSEGV
  (T-67). The original ask had a second half, "or have `process.wait` refuse an address it did
  not hand out", which is not done. Because `handle` cannot be named (138.32), every loke helper
